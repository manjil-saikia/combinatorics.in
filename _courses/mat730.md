A sequel to [MAT 631 Algebraic Combinatorics]({{ '/courses/mat631/' | relative_url }}), though the
basic objects from that course are recalled as they are needed. The goal is to understand the
representations of the symmetric group. Along the way we see a good deal of beautiful
combinatorics and a decent amount of algebra.
